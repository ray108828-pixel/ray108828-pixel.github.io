Research

My research primarily focuses on securities regulation and enforcement, examining both the determinants and consequences of regulatory oversight and enforcement actions. In particular, I study how securities regulators make oversight decisions and how their actions influence corporate behavior and capital market outcomes. My other research interests include executive characteristics, climate risk and ESG, debt contracting, and auditing.
