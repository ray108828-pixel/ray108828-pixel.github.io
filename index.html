About

I am an Associate Professor at the Beedie School of Business, Simon Fraser University. My research examines financial reporting, securities regulation and enforcement, and how executive characteristics shape corporate outcomes. Recent work studies SEC oversight and comment letters, climate-related risk in debt contracting and audit pricing, and the measurement of executive personality using machine learning.
